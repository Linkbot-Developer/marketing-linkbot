## 1. What we sell

**Linkbot Pte Ltd** is a Singapore business automation company. We help businesses streamline their operations and boost productivity by automating repetitive admin work (data entry, invoicing, reporting, reminders) using process automation, AI and system integration.

- **Website positioning:** "Business automation, done for you."
- **Main positioning:** Empowering businesses with Intelligent Automation.
- **Method, S.O.A.R.:** Simplify, Optimise, Automate, Reduce.

**Services**
1. **Business Process Automation:** repetitive tasks, notifications and workflows.
2. **AI Solutions & Integration:** ChatGPT and generative AI built into workflows.
3. **CRM & Business Management:** setting up and customising Lark and HubSpot.
4. **System Integration & API Development:** connecting apps so data flows automatically.
5. **Chatbot & Messaging Automation:** WhatsApp, Telegram, SendPulse.
6. **Digital Workflow & Collaboration:** Lark Base and Lark Suite for centralised info and visibility.
7. **Business Software Consultation:** finding inefficiencies and recommending the right tools.

**Typical project (end to end):** discovery and requirements → workflow analysis and planning → configuration, integration and build → testing and deployment → documentation, training and ongoing support. Linkbot hosts and supports the systems it builds.

**What makes us different:** practical, business-first solutions (not tech for its own sake) · flexible multi-platform integrations · customised implementations · automation combined with AI · end-to-end support from consultation through adoption.

## 3. Who to filter out

**Valid lead (confirmed 18 Nov 2026).** A lead counts towards the targets only if it meets all four:
1. It is a **business**, not an individual consumer.
2. It is in a **target segment** (A–D above).
3. It has a **real automation need**.
4. It is **contactable**, with a working email, phone, WhatsApp or DM thread.

## 4. Offer and call to action

- **Core offer:** a **free consultation**. This is the only thing we promote as free. Don't advertise workflow assessments or demos as free.
- **Prices:** never mention prices publicly unless management approves. Pricing depends on scope, complexity, subscriptions and customisation.
- **Project duration:** varies by project and is discussed during the consultation. Never state timelines in content; say "we'll scope this with you in a free consultation".
- **Approved CTAs:** Book a free consultation · Schedule a discovery call · Talk to our automation experts · Explore our business automation solutions · Visit our website · Contact us for a personalised solution · Discover how automation can improve your business · Request a product demonstration.
- **Content mix:** Educate 40% · Prove 20% · Show 20% · Engage 10% · Promote 10%. Not every post is a sales pitch.

## 6. Tone of voice

**Tone:** professional but approachable · practical and informative · a friendly technology expert · confident without being pushy · innovative but grounded in real business needs.

**Always**
- Explain tech with real business examples, in plain language for non-technical readers.
- Lead with the outcome (time saved, less manual work, better visibility), then explain how it works.
- Use clear headlines, strong hooks and practical takeaways.
- Use screenshots, workflow diagrams and demos.
- Use only verified statistics and approved client examples.

**Never**
- Use unexplained jargon.
- Promise guaranteed savings, results or timelines.
- Claim that AI replaces employees.
- Exaggerate.
- Criticise competitors by name.
- Publish confidential client information.
- Present hypothetical results as real ones.

**Words we like:** Simplify, Optimise, Automate, Streamline, Empower, Productivity, Intelligent Automation, Digital Transformation, Seamless Integration, Business Growth, Work Smarter, Efficiency, Connected Workflows, Business Insights.

**Words to avoid:** guaranteed results, zero effort, fully replaces employees, instant transformation, 100% error-free (unless technically verified), revolutionary (unless justified), best in the industry (unless there's evidence), buzzwords without context.

**Taglines**
- Supporting: *Simplify your workflows. Optimise your operations. Automate your growth.*
- Campaign: *Work smarter, not harder.*
- Educational: *Less manual work. More time for what matters.*

**Language:** British English only, on every channel. (This overrides the brief's mention of Bahasa Indonesia.)

## 7. Goals

**Targets**
- **10 new valid leads a week**, about 40 a month.
- **10 consultations booked a month.**

All marketing should drive towards these. Every post or campaign should be traceable to enquiries or consultation bookings where possible.

Aims by channel:
- **LinkedIn:** build credibility and generate business enquiries (main B2B channel).
- **Instagram / Facebook:** awareness, and making automation easy to understand visually.
- **TikTok:** awareness and discovery, only if there are resources for video.
- **Blog:** SEO and education that leads readers to consultations.
- **WhatsApp:** direct enquiries and lead conversion, plus a showcase of our messaging automation.

**Default posting schedule** (use this unless told otherwise):

| Instagram | 3 posts a week |
| Facebook | 2–3 posts a week |

## 9. Rules and compliance

- Client names, logos, screenshots and project details need **written permission**.
- No fake testimonials, invented statistics or exaggerated results.
- Never show credentials, client data or sensitive internal screens.
- Label clearly what is a real capability, a proposed feature or a concept demo.
- Verify technical details before publishing tutorials or integration demos.
- Follow third-party brand guidelines when using software logos.
- **Content approver: marketing@linkbot.sg only.** Nothing goes live without approval, and unapproved posts are held, never published. Get a technical check on claims about capabilities or integrations.
- **Automation:** specified in [docs/marketing-system-spec.md](docs/marketing-system-spec.md). Earlier design notes are in [docs/automation-design.md](docs/automation-design.md).
  - n8n runs the flow and Claude writes the copy and checks it.
  - Images and video come from free and open-source tools: Gemini or Cloudflare images, HTML templates, HyperFrames and Edge TTS.
  - n8n publishes through each platform's own API.
  - Lark Base holds the content calendar, CRM, results and settings, and Lark Drive holds the media.
  - Content is proposed at least 7 days before it posts.
  - Platforms: FB, IG, LinkedIn and TikTok, with the blog later.
  - Reports go out weekly by Lark message and monthly by email.
  - Budgets and posting volumes are set from a dashboard (a Lark Base Settings table), not hard-coded.
  - Paid ads come in a later phase.
  - The website source is at `github.com/Linkbot-Developer/linkbot-redesign`, edited by the developers.
  - The social accounts are currently new **test** accounts. Switch to the live company accounts once the flow is proven.
